About

Background & Qualifications

Advocate Prakhar Deep Mishra practises primarily before the High Court of Judicature at Allahabad, Lucknow Bench, and also appears before the District Court, Lucknow, the Board of Revenue, Uttar Pradesh, and the Commissionerate, Lucknow.

He holds a B.A., an M.A. in Public Administration and an LL.B. Originally from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, he is based in Lucknow.

Earlier in his career he worked in the education and EdTech sector, in student counselling, academic guidance and educational consulting.
